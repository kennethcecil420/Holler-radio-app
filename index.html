FM DIAL

Live radio comes from iHeartRadio and the open Radio Browser directory. Archive stations play old radio, 78s, sermons and stranger things from the Internet Archive on a live clock. AM talk stations are built from podcasts' public feeds (and 1170 carries Coast to Coast AM live overnight from an affiliate's stream), including stations that play today's top Apple Podcasts charts by genre. The DX band pulls in long-distance stations from every corner of the country, with oddities tucked between them. The WV band scans every West Virginia station in the directory, with the ones closest to Princeton first. The CB band is all Appalachian: mountain stations, Bluefield and Beckley locals, field tapes and haints. Some signals have no known source. Your lineup and podcast progress are saved on this phone.
